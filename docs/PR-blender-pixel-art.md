# Apply Blender art to the full dinosaur/building set and fix backward walks

The pilot's lifted feet moved backward while planted feet moved forward, making animals look as though they walked backward. The corrected poses move planted feet backward relative to the body and lift them during the forward swing. Left-facing animals mirror the same frame order; authored walk poses keep their planted feet grounded without an extra body bob.

All 16 dinosaur species now have four Blender-authored poses, and all 10 park buildings have Blender models and individual palettes. The shared painters apply the new art consistently to park textures, babies, catalog, guide, menu, fossil masks and regenerated PWA icons. Swimmers and flyers select all four poses. Blender art is the regular production default; development and the separate art preview retain the original comparison. Saves remain v21.

The compressed Blender sources total about 2.5 MB and stay outside the shipped build. All 74 exported frame hashes and all 12 land-species foot-contact records reproduced exactly through Blender MCP. The 26-asset comparison page supports native/4× views, babies, backgrounds, both directions and translated animation. Regeneration instructions are in `assets-source/README.md`.

Validation: 255 regular tests plus the separate performance test passed; regular and art-preview production builds passed; all 13 existing browser tours passed; targeted integration checks verified every species' four game poses in both directions, matching artwork across consumers, preserved building dimensions, pause freezing, habitat behavior and four catalog viewports. Gait regressions check positions extracted from actual foot meshes and the exported ground-contact pixels. The regular production PWA passed all 16 menu-art comparisons, v21 save creation, Pages-subpath service-worker control and saved-park resume after an offline restart. A simulation-test timeout during concurrent Chrome work was resolved by running the complete suite in isolation.

The final isolated headless comparison used 32 dinosaurs and 150 visitors at 844×390: both art sets measured 50.1 ms p95 frame intervals and about 1.3 ms p95 entity updates. Original startup was 3,383 ms; Blender was 3,328 ms. Gzipping each production file measured 586,911 bytes versus 575,457 before the pilot: 11,454 bytes extra. These single-run software-rendering observations do not establish physical Safari performance. An earlier sample overlapped a build and was excluded.

Review locally at `/scripts/art/review.html`, or play `/?quickstart&seed=7`. The owner approved the pilot and requested this full rollout. Physical iPhone/iPad Safari checks and integration review remain pending; this is a local PR draft and the branch has not been deployed.
