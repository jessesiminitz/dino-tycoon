# Dinosaur reference and anatomy review — 2026-10-05

Reviewed all 28 roster animals against web photographs of skeletons and museum
reconstructions, with additional primary research for ambiguous features. The
models remain stylized native-resolution pixel art: the references guide the
silhouette and landmark placement, not exact scale ratios or fossil-proven
colors. Generic mounts and related taxa are identified below where applicable.

26 canonical silhouettes were revised. Pteranodon received a resting-animation
attachment correction; Triceratops was retained after review. All 28 animals were
regenerated and inspected through Blender MCP. Extra horizontal camera room is
symmetric, keeping world body scale, grounded toe positions and shadow size.
Walking, idle, eating and resting clips use the same authoring geometry.

## Per-animal picture review

These links identify the pictures used for comparison, including photographs
hosted outside the exhibiting museum. They are reference links, not game assets;
source pages retain their image credits. The additional armored mount is used
only as an ankylosaur armor comparison, and the complete therizinosaur mounts
are reconstructions/related-taxon context. Historic upright Iguanodon mounts
help locate the hand and skull, rather than dictate its modern body posture.
A search hit captioned Kentrosaurus showed a different plated mount; it was
excluded and replaced with another view of the identified Berlin skeleton.
Local downloaded pictures and contact
sheets are ignored under `docs/art-review/` and are not distributed in the build.

## Research cross-checks

- [Dilophosaurus redescription and skull reconstruction (Marsh & Rowe, 2020)](https://www.jsg.utexas.edu/vpl/files/MarshandRowe2020.pdf): cranial crests and skull outline. [UT's illustrated release](https://news.utexas.edu/2020/07/07/famous-jurassic-park-dinosaur-is-less-lizard-more-bird/) credits Brian Engh's museum reconstruction.
- [Spinosaurus tail study (Ibrahim et al., 2020)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-020-2190-3) and [Hone & Holtz's comparative figures (2021)](https://www.palaeo-electronica.org/content/2021/3283-the-ecology-of-spinosaurus-figures): taper, expanded tail and sail proportions. Swimming ecology remains debated; this art pass changes no habitat rules.
- [Nigersaurus skull and skeleton (Sereno et al., 2007)](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0001230): broad transverse muzzle, short neck and low grazing reconstruction. Width across the skull does not imply a long side-view shovel.
- [Microraptor wind-tunnel reconstruction (Dyke et al., 2013)](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms3489) and [plumage reconstruction (Li et al., 2012)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22403389/): feathered forelimbs, hind limbs and distal tail. Existing game palettes are retained.
- [Mosasaur soft tissues (Lindgren et al., 2013)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24022259/): a bilobed fin with a larger lower lobe, documented in a related mosasaur; used as a clade-level reconstruction cue.
- [AMNH Dimorphodon](https://www.amnh.org/explore/ology/ology-cards/356-dimorphodon-macronyx): stocky body, bulky head, long tail and folded wings. The precise terminal tail membrane is uncertain, so the model uses a simple taper.
- [Plesiosaurus dolichodeirus holotype photographs and specimen identification](https://plesiosauria.com/directory/genera/plesiosaurus/): named specimen rather than treating every long-necked marine mount as Plesiosaurus.

## Reproduction and review

Use [assets-source/README.md](../assets-source/README.md) to export canonical
models and the full motion set, then run both converters. Pixel cleanup positions
are in unshifted modeling coordinates; the exporter adds `modelOffsetX` once.
The pterosaur resting fold likewise uses the shifted true shoulder coordinate.
New cheek meshes follow the skull through grazing and resting neck rotations.

Review all animals at http://localhost:5173/scripts/art/review.html. Inspect at
1× before enlarging, then check walk/eat/rest, both directions and baby mode.
The local same-scale sheets are `reference-before-1.png` through `-4.png` and
`reference-after-1.png` through `-4.png`; `reference-model-audit.json` records the
28 native MCP inspections. Automated checks verify attachment, clipping, ground
contacts, baby faces, mirrored gait, runtime atlases and gallery integration.
Physical iPhone/iPad review remains pending before integrating/deploying this
graphics branch. Save schema stays v21.
